Company Profile02
The problem we exist for

Most enterprise AI spend burns budget, not blockers.

The gap is rarely the model. It is the messy, high-stakes environment the model has to work inside — legacy systems, manual handoffs, and processes nobody fully owns. Four patterns account for most of the waste:

Pilots that never ship

A demo impresses in a meeting, then stalls at security review, integration, or hand-over. Budget is spent; nothing runs.

Spend without a metric

Model and compute bills grow monthly while no business number — cost, speed, conversion — is assigned or moved.

Vendors blind to legacy

The real constraints live in ERP, CRM and custom systems built over a decade. Most suppliers never learn them.

Knowledge that walks out

When the contract ends, understanding of the system leaves with the contractor — and dependency stays.

Our answer: engineers who own a business metric, working inside your systems — not around them.
